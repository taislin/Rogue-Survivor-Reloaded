Reference

Controls

Every binding below is a default. Press Shift+K in game to rebind any of them; your choices are saved between sessions. Arrow keys, WASD style movement and the numpad are also read where they do not conflict with a binding.

Two that catch everyone out

Ctrl+S is switch place with a follower; Shift+S is save. Likewise N builds a small fortification while Ctrl+N builds a large one.

Movement and time

Key Action Notes
←↓↑→ Move Moving into something occupied bumps it, which is how most interactions are triggered.
R Run Toggle. Twice as fast, and it costs stamina — you cannot start running while tired, and you stop automatically if you become tired.
. Wait / rest Do nothing. Recovers stamina faster than any other action.
W Wait one hour Interrupted if you see an enemy, get hungry or sleepy, or witness something important.
Z Sleep Recovers sleep and heals slowly — faster on a couch, anywhere at all with the Hardy skill. You cannot sleep while hungry, and you are blind and helpless until something wakes you.
S Shout Makes noise. Good for waking sleepers nearby; bad for anything else.
X Use exit Stand on a ladder, hole or stairway to change maps.
P / Ctrl+P Push / pull Shove a person or shift a movable object. Pushing costs stamina equal to the object's weight, and you move with what you push.

Fighting and fire

Key Action Notes
Bump an actor Melee attack A tiring action. Your Melee skill, any equipped melee weapon and your stamina all matter.
Bump a barricade Attack the barricade A sure-to-hit, full-damage hit that destroys the material you used.
Bump a door / window / shelf Open, take, talk Bumping is the game's universal interaction verb.
F Fire Enter fire mode, then pick a target. Press again for rapid fire: two shots for one action, each less likely to hit. The line of fire is drawn red (no shot), yellow (beyond efficient range) or green.
F with a grenade Throw Throw distance comes from the grenade and your Strong skill. It explodes a few turns later. You can throw past your own field of view, and over people.
C Close door / window Closes an adjacent one.
Shift+E Eat corpse Only in modes with corpses. Desperate, and it counts against you socially.
Shift+R Revive corpse Requires the Medic skill and a medikit.

Items and inventory

Key Action Notes
1…0 Item slot 1–10 Use, equip or unequip the item in that slot. Hold Ctrl/Shift/Alt for the modified slot bindings.
Left-click Use / equip / take / talk On your inventory, on the ground inventory, or on an actor. Equipping does not end your turn; using an item does.
Right-click Drop On one of your items. Dropping a useless item discards it.
Hover Look Over any tile, actor or item for a description — the main way to work out what something actually is.
G Give item Pick the item with the mouse, press G, then choose an adjacent actor. Ends both your turn and theirs.
B Barricade Barricade a door, window or fortification. Consumes one barricading material per use, up to a cap.
K Break / dismantle Yields material from some objects. Needs an equipped melee weapon.
N / Ctrl+N Build fortification Small (transparent, jumpable, pushable, 2 material) or large (opaque, blocks movement, 4 material). Needs Carpentry; level 3 uses one less.
A Use spray Spray paint tags a tile — and marks your minimap if the option is on. Stench spray erases the scent trail the undead follow.
Ctrl+E Mark as enemies Declare nearby actors as personal enemies. Free action, instant, and permanent. There is no undo.

Other people

Key Action Notes
Bump an actor Trade A quick, semi-random exchange. Look for the can trade icon on the map.
E Negotiate trade Choose the exchange yourself. Charismatic unlocks items they would not otherwise part with; a follower who trusts you accepts anything.
T Lead / leave Ask someone to follow you. You need the Leadership skill, and the cap is your skill level.
O Order / directive Set standing directives and one-off tasks. Both of you must see each other, or both have a tracker equipped.
Ctrl+S Switch place Swap positions with a follower.

System

Shift+SSave game
Shift+LLoad game
Shift+NScreenshot
Shift+AAbandon run
Shift+QQuit

There is one save slot. Loading does not erase the run you abandoned — but a true rogue survivor never reloads, only his weapons.

Inside menus

Menu screens — main menu, character creation, options, keybindings, hiscores, trade, orders — read the keyboard directly and do not use the bindings above.

Browser notes

Screenshots are downloaded by the browser rather than written silently to a folder, so death screenshots are off by default — turn them on in the options if you want them. The game is keyboard and mouse only; there is no touch support. Append ?debug=1 to the URL for per-action [render] and per-frame [draw] logging in the console.
